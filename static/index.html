How to read this result

Scores are interpreted on the selected benchmark’s scale. On Davis, higher pKd values indicate stronger predicted binding (values ≥ 7.0 pKd represent active/strong kinase binding). On KIBA, higher scores reflect stronger integrated bioactivity (values ≥ 12.1 indicate strong binding). Compare candidates strictly within the same dataset.

About this estimate

Research estimate, not a measured result. Predictions depend on SMILES formatting, sequence length limits, and training distribution. Experimental validation is required before drawing biological conclusions.
